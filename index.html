Detection rules

NORMAL – all readings below thresholds.
WARNING – temperature or smoke above its threshold (buzzer beeps, no SMS).
ALARM – flame detected, or temperature and smoke both above thresholds (buzzer on, SMS sent to every number, repeated after the interval while the alarm lasts).
